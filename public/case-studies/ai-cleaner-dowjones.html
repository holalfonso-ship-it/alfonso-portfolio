S

After granting access, 31% of users still quit. Cleanup felt complicated.

T

Get users to a completed clean in one session.

A

I collapsed seven screens into two: scan progress and one results screen with a single action. Decisions moved to advanced settings. I rejected progressive disclosure, which still asks for a decision at each step.

R

Secondary drop-off from 31% to under 8% in six weeks.

S

The Arabic market needed higher density, RTL layouts, Mada and STC Pay, and a different trust hierarchy.

T

Serve both markets without doubling maintenance, as the only designer.

A

I built one token layer with market overrides for density, type scale and payment UI: 120+ components, not two products.

R

A fix ships to both markets at once. Design-to-dev delivery 30% faster, sprint velocity up 25%.

Why it matters: half the maintenance debt and no divergence between markets. It should have opened the pitch.

08

What I would do differently.

01

Instrument the demo scan earlier

Our best intervention had no solid analytics until two months after launch.

02

Lead with the business case

The one-system call was a maintenance and velocity argument as much as a design one. I should have opened with it.

The 67% drop-off was not a design problem. It was a strategy problem: we asked for trust before earning it.
